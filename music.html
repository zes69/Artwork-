You in Me

You in Me is the beautiful story of John 17, where Jesus is in the garden before he is killed. This is his last desire, his last wish, his last breath ... Father, I desire that they may be ONE as we are ONE.

Wonderful Peace

Wonderful Peace is the 1st song that I ever wrote. I couldn't play the guitar very well and an angel placed my fingers on the chords and this song came out. Enjoy the beauty and fragrance of this beautiful song. The video was created by Anca ‪@BearLifeDesigns‬ and it is absolutely gorgeous. Let this video help you to release yourself into heavenly realms.

Interior Design

I wanted a 70’s look style. I could see rooms within rooms inside a person. The realm of love, the realm of consciousness, the realm of intimacy … and so on. For us to know what Father called us to, we must have been face to face with Him… it’s more like faces to faces. We encounter Father in each of these realms, in each chamber. And still… all is happening within. Anca Johnsen (video creator)
