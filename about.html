Meet SaraiB

Creativity has always been at the heart of SaraiB's journey.

As an artist, musician, author, and storyteller, SaraiB creates from a foundation of faith and intuition, heavenly inspiration, and wonder. Every painting, song, and written word reflects a life shaped by meaningful experiences and a deep desire to encourage others through beauty and authentic expression.

Her artwork invites moments of deep reflection. Her music awakens the soul. Her writing shares a journey of hope, transformation, and the extraordinary ways inspiration can be found in everyday life.

Whether you're discovering a painting that speaks to your heart, listening to a melody that brings peace, or exploring the pages of A Mystic's Journey: A Life of Encounter , you're invited to experience the world through the eyes of an artist who believes creativity has the power to inspire, heal, and connect us all.

Thank you for being part of the journey.

With gratitude — SaraiB

In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.Proverbs 3:6 · KJV

Philosophy

Live awake. Create from the heart. Share beauty. Create hope. Invite connection.

At the heart of it, I see these ideas:

Life is meant to be experienced deeply. I am drawn to quiet moments—early mornings, dreams, inspiration, reflection—when something inside me awakens. Creativity is a way of discovering myself. Painting isn’t simply something I do; it seems to be a way of listening to what is happening within me and giving it form. Color carries emotion and possibility. It is a language through which I experience and communicate life. Art, words, sound can transform people. I create to reach something in another person. There is always an invitation to awaken in the things that I create. Inspiration should be followed, allowing passion to lead rather than keeping creativity confined to a safe or conventional place. Creation is an expression of the heart. Whether through painting, music, or storytelling, I believe that authentic creation comes from within and can become a bridge between one heart and another. I believe we are here to awaken—to ourselves, to one another, and to the beauty that surrounds us.

Creativity is one of the ways we awaken. Through color, painting, music, words, and stories, I listen to what stirs within me and give it expression. I believe inspiration is something to follow, not something to ignore, and that our deepest passions can lead us toward discovering who we are meant to become.

I am obsessed with color because color speaks to me. It carries emotion, energy, mystery, joy, and possibility. Through it, I explore what words sometimes cannot express.

I believe art has the power to touch hearts, create hope, open doors, and invite connection. Every creation is an offering—a piece of my heart placed into the world with the hope that it might awaken something in someone else.

For me, creating is not simply making art.

It is a way of living awake.

Style

My creative artistic style is intuitive, expressive, and deeply connected to a heavenly dimensional perspective.

I write, paint, and sing/song-write from inspiration—sometimes from a feeling, a thought, a dream, or simply a moment when a particular idea or color speaks to me.

I am obsessed with color and fascinated by its ability to express what words cannot. Through bold color, movement, pattern, and emotion, I explore themes of awakening, peace, transformation, connection, and hope.

I don’t always begin with knowing exactly where a painting will take me. I allow the process to unfold. For me, painting is a conversation between my heart, my imagination, and the canvas.

My art, whether it is in my writing, my songs, or my paintings, is an invitation—to pause, to feel, to reflect, and perhaps to discover something within yourself.

I create what awakens within me, and I hope it awakens something within you.
